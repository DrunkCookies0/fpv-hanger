# Backlog

Every idea, request and bug, one line each, in the order the Product Manager proposes to build
them. The order is a proposal; the CEO decides. No line has a date.

**From** says where the line came from: the read-me's Coming soon, a changelog entry, or my own
reading of the app. A pilot's request, when one arrives through the CEO, is quoted as the pilot
put it. **Size** is small (a day or less for Builder), medium (a few days) or large (a week or
more, or uncertain).

The versions are the ones in `ROADMAP-v1.md`. A line with no version is after v1.0.
